# Postflight Checklist

1. Verify only allowed sheets were edited.
2. Verify only allowed columns were edited.
3. Verify no protected-sheet row was copied/moved into blocked target sheets.
4. Verify row count deltas match preflight plan.
5. Verify added and removed IDs are reported.
6. Verify QA round 1 passed.
7. Verify QA round 2 passed.
8. Verify QA round 3 passed.
9. Reopen the saved workbook from disk and verify the actual non-empty row count.
10. Reopen the saved workbook from disk and verify the actual added identifiers.
11. Verify policy compliance passed.
12. Verify a concise completion report was delivered.
